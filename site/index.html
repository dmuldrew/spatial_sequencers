  • 🌀 PyroSync Observatory

    26-Year Wildfire Spiral

    Harmonic Tension & 3D Globe Locator

    Unrolls 309 monthly steps (2001–2026) benchmarked against the 1991–2020 NASA POWER seasonal normal across six global biomes. As dryness and fire danger depart from norm (+σ), d3-audio shifts chords from warm C-major consonance into tritone dissonance.

    NASA POWER v2 · EONET v3 · FIRMS MODIS · D3.js · Tone.js · d3-audio

  • ⬡ Earth Atlas · H3

    Central Iowa MODIS Hex Map

    Surface Temperature Meets Vegetation (NDVI)

    Explore 7,443 H3 hexagons across a 180 × 120 mile Central Iowa study area prepared with raster_h3. MOD11A2 daytime land surface temperature (°C) drives hue from green to brown while MOD13Q1 NDVI controls color saturation.

    MODIS Terra · H3 v4 · Leaflet · raster_h3 Pipeline

    Space Jukebox — 3D Fire Flight

    The Ship Is the Playhead, the Map Is the Score

    A spaceship tours a 3D Earth while 330 NASA FIRMS wildfire hotspots become sound — the tone climbs over fire regions and major hotspots ping in stereo as you pass them. Narration on by default for blind and low-vision listeners, with Auto Tour + Manual Pilot (keyboard & Xbox controller).
