🎵 Space Jukebox

Hear the planet changing. Built to be listened to first — eyes optional.

How to listen

Every track turns real NASA and NOAA measurements into a melody. Pitch is always the value: when the note climbs, the number is rising. Each track has a home tone — a reference note you can return to, like a landmark. Press the button and we will talk you through it while you hear the lowest and highest notes.

Keyboard: Space play / pause · ← → step one year (hold Shift for ten) · 1 / 2 switch track · W where am I? · R home tone · S sound-first mode
