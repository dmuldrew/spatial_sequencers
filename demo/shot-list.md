Covers **all** of Spatial Sequencers' sub-projects, not just one. Record from
the **live site** on a real machine (sound matters — this is an audio project):
<https://dmuldrew.github.io/spatial_sequencers/>

Total runtime must stay **at or under 30.0 seconds**.

| # | Time | On screen | What you hear | How |
|---|------|-----------|---------------|-----|

**End card text** (a plain slide or a title in your editor):

> Spatial Sequencers — Earth data you can hear.

## Recording steps

1. Use a machine with working speakers/audio — record **system audio**, not the
   microphone. Windows: Xbox Game Bar (Win+G) captures app audio; OBS works too.
2. Record each shot separately at 1080p; trim and join in Clipchamp or the
   Photos app. Separate takes are easier than one perfect run.
3. Keep the joined video **≤ 30.0s** — check the final file's duration before
   uploading.
4. Export MP4 (H.264). Upload somewhere public with no login wall (YouTube
   unlisted, Vimeo, or a direct file link) and paste the link into the
   submission.

## If the sound misbehaves on the day

Submit the slide deck (`slides.html`, 7 slides) instead — the rules allow
either. The deck tells the same whole-project story with stills and stats.
