# Demo

Submission demo assets for **Spatial Sequencers** (NASA Space Apps Challenge
2026). The submission rules allow **one** of: a video up to 30 seconds, or up
to 7 slides — public link, no login required.

- **`slides.html`** — the 7-slide deck, covering the whole project (PyroSync,
  Space Jukebox, the Midwest Fire Timeline, and the combined flyby). Open it
  in any browser; navigate
  with the arrow keys, spacebar, or click (right side = next). It also prints
  one slide per page if you need a PDF.
- **`shot-list.md`** — the plan for the 30-second video version: six timed
  shots, what should be on screen and audible in each, recording steps, and
  the end card text.
- **`img/`** — a looping ~5-second GIF of every sub-project (PyroSync's
  spiral, the Space Jukebox flight, the Midwest timeline, the Combined
  Flyby), captured from the real apps, plus stills from the flight and the
  team icon.
