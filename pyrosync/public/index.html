PyroSync methods and monthly records

Communicating Climate Departure with Harmonic Tension

Rather than mapping raw seasonal temperature alone—where normal summers always sound intense—PyroSync measures each month from 2001 to 2026 against its biome's 30-year 1991–2020 calendar-month baseline using standardized z-scores (σ) of Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) and Fosberg Fire Weather Index (FFWI). Normal variability remains consonant; statistically significant positive departures drive d3-audio's scaleTension() into suspended, minor, and tritone dissonance with microtonal detuning and FM grit.

NASA Multi-Decade Datasets (1991–2026)

  • NASA POWER v2 (1991–2026): 36 years of monthly 2m max temperature, relative humidity, 10m wind, and precipitation. Years 1991–2020 form the climatological normal; 2001–2026 unroll around the spiral.
  • NASA EONET v3 (2015–2026): Multi-year wildfire event detections joined by calendar month to spotlight confirmed outbreaks along extreme +σ spiral arms.
  • NASA FIRMS MODIS C6.1: Recent 7-day active hotspots & Fire Radiative Power (MW) plus on-demand 24h refresh rendered directly on the central 3D globe.

Reading the Spiral & Central Globe

Each full revolution represents one Jan → Dec seasonal year advancing outward from 2001 (inner ring) to 2026 (outer ring). Angular position marks the month, ribbon width/glow encode VPD/FFWI departure (σ) from the 1991–2020 normal, amber/crimson flares mark EONET outbreaks (2015+), and the central globe rotates to frame the active biome alongside live MODIS satellite fire detections.
